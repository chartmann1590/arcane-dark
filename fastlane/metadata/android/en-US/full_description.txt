Your AI Dungeon Master lives on your phone.

Arcane Dark is a cozy, torch-lit D&D adventure you can play anywhere — solo and private, or with up to 5 friends around one table. No subscriptions. No waiting. Your story, your way.

YOUR STORY STAYS PRIVATE
Solo quests live only on your device — never uploaded, never sold. Play without an account if you like. That's the whole point of an on-device Dungeon Master.

ADVENTURE ANYWHERE — NO SIGNAL NEEDED
After a quick one-time setup, solo works fully offline. Perfect for planes, cabins, or the couch.

MAKE A HERO YOU LOVE
Pick ancestry, calling, and background, then shape looks step-by-step with a live portrait preview. Many races, classes, and point-buy the tabletop way.

A NEW DUNGEON EVERY TIME
Ever-changing maps with cozy fog-of-war — explore at your own pace. Never the same twice.

BRING YOUR FRIENDS
Host or join — up to 6 share the same map and story. Invite with a code or QR.

HOW IT FEELS TO PLAY
1. Make your hero — who you are, how you fight, what you carry.
2. Step into the dark — say what you do: "I inspect the altar," "I try to talk them down."
3. Watch the story answer — your AI Dungeon Master replies line by line, dice and all.

PRIVACY, PLAIN AND SIMPLE
Solo = offline & private. Multiplayer only shares what the table needs to stay in sync (and you can delete any hero or campaign with a tap). See the privacy policy in-app under Settings → Privacy Policy and on the website.

FREE TO START, FAIR TO PLAY
Jump in for free — no paywalls on your story. Ads help keep the lights on, and you're in control of your preferences.
