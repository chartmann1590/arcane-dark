Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026 • Plain-English version of PRIVACY.md • ← Back to Arcane Dark • Coming soon to Google Play

The short version

When you play alone, your story never leaves your phone. We don’t see it, store it, or sell it. The app only uses the internet for three things you choose: a one-time setup download, syncing a multiplayer table you join, and showing ads to keep the lights on.

Solo play — private by design

Everything about your solo games — your hero, your choices, the story the Dungeon Master tells — is made and saved only on your device. No upload, anywhere. After the first quick setup you can play fully offline and nothing about your adventure reaches us or anyone else.

Playing with friends

If you host or join a shared table, the bits needed to keep everyone together — like party stats, where you are on the map, quest progress, and the messages you send at that table — are saved with Google Firebase so friends in your party can see them. This is linked to a simple anonymous account (we never ask for your name or email). It’s kept only as long as your party exists, plus a short window for fixing bugs.

Ads

Arcane Dark shows ads from Google AdMob to support development. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID to show ads. If you’re in the UK or European Economic Area you’ll see a clear consent screen the first time you open the app, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy Choices. We don’t control what AdMob does beyond Google’s own policies.

Crash and speed info

We use Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring to spot bugs and slow spots. They send only technical details — like device model, OS version, app version, and which screen had a problem — never your campaign text or anything you typed to the storyteller.

Children

Arcane Dark isn’t made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect info from children. Ads are not set up for child-directed use.

You’re in control

  • Play solo, offline — nothing leaves your device.
  • Change ad choices anytime in Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Delete any hero or campaign with a tap — gone from your device right away.
  • Uninstall the app to clear all local data and the setup download.

Other services we use

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, syncing tables, crash & performance)
  • Google AdMob (ads)
  • Hugging Face (one-time download of the open story model)

Each has its own privacy policy for how they handle data.

Changes

If this changes in a meaningful way we’ll update the date above and note it on the store listing.

Questions?

Use the contact on the store page when it’s live, or open an issue on GitHub — we read it.

This is a plain-language summary for players. If you build your own version, please have a lawyer review your policy and update the contact details.
