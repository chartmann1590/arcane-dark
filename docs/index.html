Questions, answered

Good to know before you roll.

Do I need internet to play? +

No — not for solo. After your first quick setup, solo adventures work fully offline. Great for flights, cabins, or anywhere without a signal. Multiplayer does need a connection to keep everyone in sync.

Is my story private? +

Yes. Solo stories stay only on your phone — never uploaded. That’s the whole point of an on-device Dungeon Master.

Can I play with friends? +

Absolutely. Host or join a party of up to 6. Share a short code or QR and you’ll all see the same dungeon and story.

When is it on Google Play? +

Coming soon to Google Play for Android — iOS is planned for later. Follow the GitHub repo and this site will show the store link the moment it’s live.

Is it free? +

Yes — free with friendly ads that keep the tavern candles lit. You can manage choices anytime in Settings.

I have never played anything like this. Can I still play? +

Oh yes — you are exactly who it is for. Just say what you would do in plain words and your Dungeon Master handles the rules, dice, and story. You will feel clever in minutes.

What do I actually do moment to moment? +

Wander, look, talk, sneak, bluff, befriend, barter, flee bravely, pet the tavern hound. Try “I listen at the door,” “I offer them my bread,” “I run — elegantly.”
