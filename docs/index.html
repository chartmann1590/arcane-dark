Why Arcane Dark is different

A real table, inside your phone.

No subscriptions. No cloud story storage. No “AI credits.” Just you, your dice, and a Dungeon Master that never phones home in solo play.

On-Device AI Dungeon Master

Powered by Google’s Gemma 4 E2B via LiteRT-LM. The model runs locally — your story text, character details, and choices never leave your phone in solo mode.

  • Gemma 4 • LiteRT-LM
  • Streaming tokens
  • Private by design

Play anywhere, no signal needed

After a one-time ~2.6GB download, solo mode works fully offline — on a plane, underground, camping, wherever. Stored in private app storage.

  • Offline solo
  • Wi-Fi confirmation
  • Resumable download

A real character creator

Pick a race, class, background, allocate ability scores the tabletop way, and build a look with a live portrait editor. Every hero feels yours.

  • Races + Classes
  • Point-buy abilities
  • Portrait builder

Procedurally generated dungeons

Every playthrough drops you into a freshly generated map — rooms, corridors, doors, hazards — no two runs alike. Explore with fog-of-war.

  • Seeded generator
  • 18×18 rooms
  • Fog of war

Bring your friends

Host or join a party of up to six and adventure together in a shared session. Invite with a code or QR — Firebase keeps everyone in sync.

  • Up to 6 players
  • QR + invite code
  • Shared world state

Private & respectful

Solo play never leaves your device. Multiplayer only syncs what the party needs. Ads via AdMob with EEA consent, and you can manage it in Settings.

  • Local-only solo
  • Ad consent controls
  • Full privacy policy

How it works

Three steps to your legend.

1

Forge a hero

Choose race, class, background, and portrait. Point-buy your abilities the tabletop way — every stat matters when the dice roll.

2

Enter the dungeon

Pick a campaign seed or let the generator roll you a new map. Move with the D-pad, inspect, talk, attack, roll — the AI narrates it all.

3

Play your way

Solo offline on the trail, or host a party. Your campaign persists on-device; delete a character or campaign anytime.

Privacy, plain and simple

Solo stays on your phone. Period.

Solo campaigns are generated and stored only on-device. Multiplayer syncs only the party state needed to keep everyone together. Ads are via AdMob with EEA consent controls.

Read the full policy PRIVACY.md on GitHub
✓
Solo = offline & local-only
After the one-time download, no solo story text is ever uploaded — not even to us.
◈
Multiplayer = minimal sync
Only character stats, position, quest progress, and session messages — tied to an anonymous account.
◎
You’re in control
Manage ad consent, delete characters/campaigns, or uninstall to wipe all local data and the model.
For developers

Built in Flutter. Designed to be forked.

Feature-organized, documented phase-by-phase in plan/. Replace placeholder AdMob IDs and drop in your own Firebase project to ship your own build.

Stack

Flutter 3.35+ / Dart 3.9+ • Riverpod • go_router • LiteRT-LM Kotlin bridge (LlmEngine.kt) • Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Crashlytics, Performance) • AdMob • Hugging Face Gemma download.

Quick start

flutter pub get
flutter run
Model (~2.6GB) downloads on first run per assets/model_manifest.json. Needs a real Android device (API 26+).

Before you ship

Swap AdMob IDs in lib/services/ad_config.dart + manifests, add your google-services.json, and follow the release checklist in plan/09-testing-polish-launch.md.
